# Build Log — [PRODUCT_NAME]

> **What this is:** the story behind the product, captured *as it's built* — the thinking, the decisions, the dead-ends, the things almost built and killed. GitHub records *what* was made; this records *what I was thinking*. It's the raw material the Sunny PM content project turns into LinkedIn/Substack articles and Instagram reels — so capture the honest version, not the tidy one.
>
> **How to use it:** append dated notes to the right bucket as you go — a line or two is plenty, don't polish. The buckets follow the loop **Spot → Scope → Build → Ship → See → Shift** (Think → Do → Learn → Change). Claude appends here automatically while building (see the build-log rule in `CLAUDE.md`); add your own lines whenever something's worth remembering.

## 2. Scope — what's worth building

*The judgment. Smallest meaningful version. What I almost built. What I killed and why. This is the most valuable bucket — it's the thinking nobody else shows.*

Cues: What's the smallest version that's still meaningful? What did I decide **not** to build? What did I almost build, then cut? Did I nearly kill the whole thing — why did it survive (or why didn't it)? What did scanning Reddit / GitHub / existing tools show?

## 3. Build — making it real

*Key decisions and why. Where AI helped or failed. The ugly first version. The dead-ends.*

Cues: What did I choose, and why — especially where I picked simple over complex? Where did AI speed me up, and where did it waste my time? Screenshot the moment it first *worked* and the moment it first *broke*. What was harder than expected?

## 4. Ship — putting it out

*What I shipped vs cut at the last minute. What launch looked like. First reactions.*

Cues: What made the cut, and what got dropped? Where did I actually put it? What were the first exact words anyone said about it?

- [YYYY-MM-DD]

## 5. See — what reality said

*Evidence, gathered over time. Who used it, what they actually did, what surprised me. This keeps filling in for weeks — change doesn't happen on launch day.*

Cues: Who used it, and what did they actually do (vs what I hoped)? What surprised me? What contradicted an assumption I held? Any real numbers, however small?

## 6. Shift — what changed

*What I changed, simplified, expanded, or killed after seeing reality — and why. This is the Change beat that lands later.*

Cues: What did I change or cut after launch? What would I do differently next time? Honest final status — done, parked, or dead?
